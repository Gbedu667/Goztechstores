Refund Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026  •  Goztech Stores ("Goztech Ventures", BN 8938182)

This policy explains how returns, refunds and exchanges work when you buy from Goztech Stores, whether in store, via WhatsApp or by delivery.

1. Where purchases happen

All purchases are completed directly with us (in store, on WhatsApp, by phone or by email). This website takes no payment and is not part of the transaction itself.

2. Inspect before you accept

  • In store / pickup: please test and inspect your device fully before leaving the store check the screen, cameras, charging port, speakers, network/SIM, IMEI and that the device is unlocked as agreed.
  • Delivery: please inspect and test your device on the spot, before the delivery is completed where possible.

Once you have accepted and tested a device as working, used-device sales are final except as set out below.

3. Faulty or misdescribed devices

  • If a device develops a fault that was not disclosed to you, or is materially different from what we agreed (wrong model, wrong storage, locked when sold as unlocked, etc.), contact us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of receiving it with a description and, where possible, a photo or video of the issue.
  • After we verify the issue, we will offer one of the following at your choice: a repair, a replacement (subject to stock).
  • Faults caused by damage after delivery (drops, liquid, unauthorised repair, software tampering) are not covered.

4. New / sealed devices

Brand-new, sealed devices carry the manufacturer's warranty where applicable. If a sealed device is faulty, we will help you claim under the manufacturer warranty, and where the manufacturer remedy is not reasonably available to you, we will work with you on a fair solution in line with applicable consumer law.

5. Accessories

Unused, unopened accessories in original packaging may be exchanged within 7 days of purchase with proof of purchase. Opened or used accessories cannot be returned for hygiene and functionality reasons, unless faulty accessories will be replaced or refunded.

6. What we cannot refund

  • Change of mind after you have tested and accepted a used device;
  • Devices damaged, unlocked improperly, repaired by a third party, or flashed with unofficial software after purchase;
  • Loss of personal data, you are responsible for backing up and wiping your own data. We can advise on how.

7. How to start a return or complaint

  1. Message us on WhatsApp (wa.link/sm2r35) or email techgoz140@gmail.com with your name, what you bought, when, and the issue.
  2. We will respond with next steps, which may include bringing or sending the device (with its accessories and proof of purchase) to the store for verification.
  3. Approved refunds are paid using the same method you paid with, within a reasonable time after verification.

8. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy removes or limits your rights under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 or any other applicable Nigerian consumer protection law. If you are not satisfied with how we handle a complaint, you may contact the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Commission (FCCPC).
