We keep enquiry and sales records only as long as needed to complete your purchase, handle any after-sales issue, and satisfy legal, tax or accounting requirements of a registered business in Nigeria, after which they are deleted or securely destroyed.

Because this website collects almost nothing, the main risk to your data sits with the channels you choose (WhatsApp, email). We protect business records with reasonable technical and organisational measures and limit access to the business owner.
